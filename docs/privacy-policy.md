# Privacy Policy for Stochastic Strength

**Last updated: June 5, 2026**

## Overview

Stochastic Strength is a personal strength training app. Your workout data stays on your device. We do not operate servers, collect analytics, or transmit any personal information.

## Data Collected and Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database:

- **Workout history** — exercises performed, sets, reps, and weights
- **Progression data** — per-muscle baseline weights and feedback
- **Exercise library** — the list of exercises and their configurations

This data never leaves your device except as described in the Strava Export section below.

## Location

The app requests access to your device's GPS to detect which gym you are at. This is used solely to filter the equipment available at your current location so the app can generate an appropriate workout. Location data is not stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere.

## Strava Export (Optional)

If you choose to export a workout to Strava, the app will ask you to authorize access to your Strava account via Strava's official OAuth flow. The export is:

- **Entirely user-initiated** — nothing is sent without your explicit action
- **One-way** — the app only writes the selected workout; it does not read any Strava data
- **Not automatic** — no background sync occurs

You can revoke the app's Strava access at any time through your Strava account settings.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not include any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services.

## Data Retention and Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all data at any time by uninstalling the app. We have no copies of your data and cannot delete it on your behalf.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
